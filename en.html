Important: your personal calendar can work locally. When you enable online synchronization, your name, shared shifts, comments, and technical connection data are sent to the server so your group members can synchronize their calendars.

1. Who is responsible for data processing?

This Privacy Policy describes how the Grafik Smen / Shift Schedule app for iOS and Android processes information to provide a personal shift calendar and optional online synchronization.

Developer and privacy contact: Ilya Yuzhakov, grafik.smen.app@gmail.com.

2. Information we process

  • Personal schedule: workdays and days off, shift types, selected colors, notes, and reminder preferences. These are used for the calendar, widget, and reminders.
  • Shared calendar (when connected): a name or nickname you provide, group membership and connection details, shift schedules, selected colors, group comments, join requests, and invitations. This information is transmitted to the synchronization server so connected members can access it.
  • Technical information: identifiers needed to register and secure a connection, session and app-attestation information, push notification tokens, and technical request records (such as IP address and request time) needed for connectivity, security, and troubleshooting.
  • Manually added coworkers: names, colors, and schedules entered by you to appear in the shared calendar, even when those coworkers do not use the service.

A telephone number or email address is not required to use your personal calendar. If you contact support, we process the contact details and information you choose to send.

3. Why we use information

We process information to display and edit shifts, connect devices and group members, synchronize the shared calendar, deliver notifications, secure account access, provide widgets, and respond to support requests. We do not use shift or comment data for the sale of personal data or targeted advertising.

Online synchronization requires an internet connection. The personal calendar can be used without a continuous internet connection.

4. Other members and service providers

Other members of your connected group can see information you share in the shared calendar, including schedules and posted comments. Please do not share information you do not want your group members to see.

Remote notifications may be delivered through Apple Push Notification service (APNs) for iOS and Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) for Android. The iOS registration security flow may use Apple App Attest. These services are governed by the applicable Apple and Google privacy terms. We use them to provide app functionality, not advertising.

This policy website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information when you visit this page, as explained in its privacy statement.

5. Device permissions and Apple Calendar

The app may ask for permission to send notifications about shifts and changes to shared calendars. On iOS, exporting your own shifts into Apple Calendar is optional and requires calendar permission. Calendar access is not required when export is disabled.

If enabled, the app creates and updates shift events in a separate calendar on your device. Any further synchronization of those events through iCloud or another calendar account is controlled by the device settings and the relevant calendar provider.

On Android, certain optional features, such as voice input if available, may use additional system permissions. You can change optional permissions in device settings.

6. Storage and security

Local information is stored on your device. Connected shared-calendar information is stored on the synchronization server for as long as needed to operate the connection and group. Connections use HTTPS, and access to shared information is restricted by authorization and group membership.

Technical logs and backups may be kept for a limited operational period for security, recovery, and troubleshooting. Residual backup copies are removed as backup sets rotate. Uninstalling the app does not automatically remove all server-side data.

7. Your choices and data deletion

  • You can edit or delete your own calendar records and comments using the actions available in the app.
  • To stop online synchronization, open Settings → Calendar Synchronization → Delete Connection (labels may differ slightly by platform or language).
  • If the app offers a synchronization account deletion option, use it to request deletion of the account and associated data. Simply uninstalling the app is not the same as requesting server-side deletion.
  • For additional access, correction, or deletion requests, email grafik.smen.app@gmail.com. Identify the app and explain your request. Do not email passwords, authentication tokens, or invitation codes.

Shared calendar records are visible to other connected members. Removing your own connection does not necessarily remove information they have already received or independently saved.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if app features or data practices change. The current version will be posted at this URL with its updated date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning your information, contact grafik.smen.app@gmail.com.
